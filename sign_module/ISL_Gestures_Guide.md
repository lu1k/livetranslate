# Comprehensive Indian Sign Language (ISL) Gesture Guide

Your Live Translate System has been heavily upgraded to recognize **Standard Indian Sign Language (ISL)**!
In ISL, most alphabets require **both hands**. The system will track Hand 1 and Hand 2 to detect these interactions.

## 🤝 Two-Handed Alphabets
*   **A**: Touch the tip of your right index finger to the tip of your left thumb.
*   **B**: Form an 'O' shape with both hands (fingertips touching thumbs) and place the hands together.
*   **D**: Hold your left index finger straight up. Form an 'O' shape with your right hand and touch the base of the left index finger.
*   **E**: Hold up both index fingers and touch their tips together.
*   **F**: Extend the index and middle fingers on both hands. Place the right fingers horizontally across the left fingers.
*   **G**: Make two fists and stack one on top of the other.
*   **H**: Hold both hands flat (palms open). Slide your right palm over your left palm.
*   **I**: Extend just your right pinky finger and touch it to your open left palm.
*   **J**: (Mapped similarly to I statically; in real ISL, draw a 'J' on the palm).
*   **K**: Extend both index fingers. Hook your right index finger horizontally against your straight left index finger.
*   **L**: Form an 'L' shape (index and thumb) with your right hand and place it on your open left palm.
*   **M**: Touch your right index, middle, and ring fingers to your open left palm.
*   **N**: Touch your right index and middle fingers to your open left palm.
*   **P**: Form an 'O' shape with your right hand and touch it to the tip of your straight left index finger.
*   **Q**: Form an 'O' shape with your right hand and touch it to your straight left thumb.
*   **R**: Cross your right index finger over your left index finger (like an X).
*   **S**: Hook your right pinky finger with your left pinky finger.
*   **T**: Touch the tip of your right index finger to the bottom edge (wrist-side) of your left palm.
*   **U**: Hold up your right Index and Middle fingers tightly closed together.ose together) and touch your left palm.
*   **V**: Point your right index and middle fingers straight (separated, like a V) and touch your left palm.
*   **W**: Interlock the fingers of both open hands.
*   **X**: Cross both index fingers over each other at the middle joints.
*   **Y**: Touch your right index finger to the base joint of your straight left index finger.
*   **Z**: Touch both open palms together horizontally (one hand pointing left, one pointing right).

## ☝️ One-Handed Alphabets
*   **C**: Curl your fingers and thumb to form a large 'C' shape.
*   **O**: Touch the tips of all your fingers to your thumb tip to form an 'O' shape.

## 📜 Punctuation & Special Symbols
*   **Space ( )**: Hold up your **Index and Middle fingers** (like a "Peace Sign") with one hand. 
*   **Backspace (Undo)**: Extend just your **pinky finger** straight up on one hand.
*   **Comma (,)**: Hook your index finger halfway down (like a claw).
*   **Question Mark (?)**: Make a fist and point your thumb straight down.
*   **Exclamation Mark (!)**: Point your index finger straight down towards the floor.

## 👋 Simple Words / Greetings
*   **HELLO / HI**: Hold one open hand up high (fingers spread wide) as if you are waving.
*   **THANK YOU** (Logs as THX): Hold a flat hand horizontally close to your chin/neck level.
